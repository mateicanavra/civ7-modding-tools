# Product Authority Update Protocol

Use this protocol when accepted actor outcomes, capability owners, result
meaning, consumer gates, or proof boundaries change. Do not use it for routine
project status updates.

## First Decide What Changed

- **Implementation correction:** behavior moves toward existing sealed
  authority. Update source, consumers, tests, and any stale durable guidance;
  do not edit the product model merely to describe progress.
- **Evidence correction:** a source, generated, installation, loader, or live
  fact changes. Update the exact evidence record and claims only.
- **Product authority change:** an actor outcome, semantic owner, non-owner,
  result meaning, public promise, or proof law changes. Update the owning sealed
  or promoted authority before dependent implementation.
- **Overlay correction:** this skill routes or summarizes the accepted model
  incorrectly. Repair the overlay without copying project status.

## Update Workflow

1. Name the Actor, Role, Goal, Task or Question affected.
2. Identify the exact old and proposed fact writer, explicit non-owners, and
   owner-local result.
3. Trace definition, realization, construction, projection, and proof effects
   using `flow-set.md`.
4. Classify current source and consumers as evidence, not authority.
5. Open a consumer gate for every public surface or package identity affected.
6. Resolve structural placement with `civ7-architecture-authority`.
7. Update the owning sealed/canonical authority and record the decision using
   `assets/authority-change-note.md` in that artifact or its governed
   workstream.
8. Update `capability-map.md`, `flow-set.md`, `policy-map.md`, or this skill
   only when a durable routing guardrail changed.
9. Run owner, projection, consumer, and proof checks.
10. Close with newly allowed claims, still-forbidden claims, and deletion or
    re-entry conditions.

## Required Authority-Change Fields

- Actor outcome and Task/Question.
- Fact, policy, transition, correction, or result changing.
- Previous writer and proposed sole writer.
- Explicit non-owners.
- Definition, production-realization, Studio-realization, Controller/Play, and
  MapGen-runs distinctions affected.
- Public surfaces and consumers.
- Exact owner result/error vocabulary.
- Independent proof classes required.
- Displaced facade, convenience path, or mixed owner to delete.
- Controlling sealed/canonical source and falsifier.

## Stop Conditions

Stop and reopen the owning model when:

- two capabilities would write the same fact or correction;
- a current path or compatibility concern is the only reason for an owner;
- Controller and Play meaning would merge;
- production and Studio ephemeral realization would share one product outcome;
- an adapter receipt would become the MapGen-runs semantic result;
- a public surface would rewrite refusal, uncertainty, or proof meaning;
- generated, installed, loader, and live facts would collapse;
- upstream Habitat or vendor mechanics are being used to manufacture Civ7
  product policy.

## Overlay Rule

This skill is a durable application overlay. It may summarize stable owner
boundaries and route to the sealed packet. It must not carry release pins,
readiness/admission/migration tables, transient proof status, workstream
progress, or copied generic vendor mechanics.

# Play Strategy

Use this to choose among options that the current play capability has already
declared lawful. Patch-sensitive mechanics, costs, caps, and exact bonuses must
come from live reads, tooltips, and current official/static policy, not this
reference.

## Decision Order

For each choice:

1. Honor explicit human intent.
2. Avoid irreversible or diplomatic escalation without consultation.
3. Satisfy current blockers and prevent waste.
4. Advance the chosen age/victory plan.
5. Preserve economy, happiness, defense, and future flexibility.
6. Prefer actions whose outcome can be reconciled reliably.

## Default Unsteered Posture

When the human gives no direction:

- play a peaceful expansion/builder game;
- choose one primary legacy/victory path from current leader/civ/map strengths
  and keep one secondary path available;
- stay within the live settlement-cap guidance;
- keep research, culture, cities, towns, and units productive;
- spend resources when they unlock compounding value rather than hoarding;
- defend borders and avoid initiating war;
- consult before a major irreversible fork.

Do not hard-code Science as the universal path. Use the live progress dashboard,
leader/civ bonuses, terrain, neighbors, and available options.

## Research And Culture

Prefer nodes that:

- unlock immediate terrain/resource improvements;
- remove a current production, expansion, defense, or happiness bottleneck;
- advance the declared legacy/victory plan;
- open high-value follow-up nodes;
- can be completed before an age transition makes them poor investments.

Use current option and progress reads. Queue deeper targets only when the public
play surface can validate the path.

## Settlements And Cities

Evaluate sites by a portfolio, not one yield:

- fresh water and growth;
- productive workable tiles;
- distinct resources and strategic access;
- defensibility and reinforcement distance;
- overlap with existing settlements;
- relationship to the live settlement cap and happiness;
- contribution to the chosen path.

Strategic settlement recommendations identify goals. A unit movement read
provides the immediate reachable action. Do not treat a recommended site as a
validated one-step coordinate.

For production, prioritize the current bottleneck:

- exploration and contact when information is scarce;
- growth/economy when expansion is constrained;
- defense when threatened;
- settlers only when capacity and escort support them;
- path-specific buildings, wonders, or projects when the timing is defensible.

Use only candidates returned by the current city view.

## Units And Fronts

- Keep civilians protected and moving toward validated goals.
- Explore along high-information routes while preserving retreat options.
- Concentrate military force instead of feeding isolated units into danger.
- Use commanders and formations according to current live capabilities and
  planning reads.
- Prefer defensive posture when combat outcome or diplomacy intent is unclear.
- Re-read after every move; path-shortfall or changed state may alter the plan.

Front summaries and target candidates are planning evidence, not mutation
authority.

## Diplomacy And Notifications

- Read the complete decision and cost/reward context.
- Prefer cooperative/neutral responses when unsteered.
- Do not declare war, spend a scarce strategic currency, or accept a severe
  obligation without human direction unless the session goal clearly requires
  it.
- Acknowledge or dismiss only through the named play action for that decision.
- Keep informational cleanup separate from mandatory decision blockers.

## Age Transitions

As an age approaches its transition:

- avoid long investments whose value will expire;
- finish achievable milestones;
- favor durable carryover value identified by current game facts;
- preserve defensive stability and currencies needed for the transition;
- consult the human on civilization or legacy choices when strategically
  significant.

Do not rely on a static list of what carries across ages; inspect current live
and official evidence.

## Resource Allocation

Spend when it creates compounding value or clears a blocker:

- Gold: critical purchase, upgrade, settlement conversion, or emergency.
- Influence: relationships and actions aligned with the plan.
- Attribute points: leader path synergy and present bottlenecks.
- Resource slots: settlement fit and current yield/strategy needs.

Retain a reserve when a known near-term decision requires it. Otherwise,
indefinite hoarding is usually lost tempo.

## Consultation Triggers

Ask the human before:

- war or a binding diplomatic commitment;
- a civilization/legacy/victory-path pivot;
- settling beyond a live cap or accepting severe empire-wide penalties;
- spending a scarce resource on near-equal alternatives;
- deleting/abandoning a valuable unit or settlement plan;
- any high-impact action whose postcondition cannot be reconciled.

## Strategy Record

For a major decision, retain only session-local reasoning:

- current situation and options;
- selected plan and why;
- key risks/consultation status;
- exact returned candidate values;
- result and next action.

Do not turn session history into durable skill content.

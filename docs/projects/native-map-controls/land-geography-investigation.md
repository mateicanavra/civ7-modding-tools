## Frame And Objective

Determine which missing land-geographic input/process is sufficiently supported
to justify one general thermal-model change. The outcome is coherent variation
among generated locations at the same latitude, not a fitted biome percentage.
Foreground: low-relief thermal residuals, temporal response and geographic
exposure. Physical-height qualification remains a separate obligation.

Hard core: one `thermalField -> climateIndices` lineage; current domain/SDK and

general procedural behavior without Earth fixture inputs or runtime fitting.
Exterior: native rendering/control, river movement, whole-planet weather,
geological coefficient search, a new artifact publication mechanism, and
temperature noise or quotas. A height-only cure is rejected by the
[source-row comparison](earth-thermal-reference.md#source-latitude-variance-follow-through),
not by a preference for a more complicated model.

## Questions And Falsifiers

**Primary:** do height-matched monthly residuals track directional ocean exposure
and ocean-land temperature contrast strongly enough to support a maritime
thermal mechanism over the strongest rival?

**Secondary:** how much is annual mean, seasonal amplitude or phase; do onshore,
offshore and interior populations differ; does the signal survive withheld
regions and retain the original lowland holdout's row-mean error?

**Rival:** heterogeneous radiative/land-surface response or the 2 m-air versus
surface-proxy comparison could explain the remaining variation. Distance to
coast alone cannot adjudicate this. No directional signal, inconsistent signs
across months/regions, or improvement only in a pooled variance statistic
falsifies a claimed maritime solution. Preserve that outcome rather than
increasing gain until a target passes.

**Exclusions:** do not ask which metre multiplier passes the generated 1 C floor,
how to make screenshots attractive, or whether a native shoreline proves Earth
physics. Keep aridity/category and smoothing findings on their separate axis.

## First Discriminator: Nonselection

The matched NOAA monthly U/V and skin-temperature references are admitted for
external reference analysis, not recipe inputs. The sealed study retains exact
source pins, Gaussian grid/calendar joins, the original 411 cells and 196/215
split, and a separate 136-cell immediate-coastal cohort. Skin temperature in
this derived file is already Celsius. Monthly mean wind and a static marine
mask do not establish time-resolved heat transport or open-water SST everywhere.

The predeclared 500/1000 km directional supports do not select a production
maritime law. After source-row/height/month controls, all-lowland exposure
correlations are +0.0449/+0.1057; the potential cold-marine exclusion gives
-0.0449/+0.0239. The original holdout has no matched coastal onshore/offshore
strata. Added-coast pooled correlations +0.2062/+0.3505 collapse to
+0.0019/-0.0806 when Europe/Africa/west-Asia is withheld. Support and regional
signs matter more than the attractive pooled association.

Changing the reference convention from 2 m air to skin does not explain away
the lowland residual: held annual row-height RMS is 1.7414 C for air and
1.7439 C for skin; annual prediction error increases rather than disappears.
This does not reject heterogeneous local surface-energy forcing, because skin
minus air is not an energy-budget measurement.

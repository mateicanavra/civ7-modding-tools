# Low-Relief Geographic Thermal Response

## Brief Identity And Readiness

Prepared 2026-09-30 by the workstream coordinator. This ready investigation
brief adapts the Investigation Design template; the committed frame is
[the continuation sequence](continuation-sequence.md). Inquiry is complete:
the user delegates Earthlike causal repair, while real Earth remains a
test-only reference. This brief selects the next discriminator, not a thermal
implementation or another approval gate.

## Frame And Objective

Determine which missing land-geographic input/process is sufficiently supported
to justify one general thermal-model change. The outcome is coherent variation
among generated locations at the same latitude, not a fitted biome percentage.
Foreground: low-relief thermal residuals, temporal response and geographic
exposure. Physical-height qualification remains a separate obligation.

Hard core: one `thermalField -> climateIndices` lineage; current domain/SDK and
Habitat authority; source-pinned observations; held evaluation identities;
general procedural behavior without Earth fixture inputs or runtime fitting.
Exterior: native rendering/control, river movement, whole-planet weather,
geological coefficient search, a new artifact publication mechanism, and
temperature noise or quotas. A height-only cure is rejected by the
[source-row comparison](earth-thermal-reference.md#source-latitude-variance-follow-through),
not by a preference for a more complicated model.

## Questions And Falsifiers

**Primary:** do height-matched monthly residuals track directional ocean exposure
and ocean-land temperature contrast strongly enough to support a maritime
thermal mechanism over the strongest rival?

**Secondary:** how much is annual mean, seasonal amplitude or phase; do onshore,
offshore and interior populations differ; does the signal survive withheld
regions and retain the original lowland holdout's row-mean error?

**Rival:** heterogeneous radiative/land-surface response or the 2 m-air versus
surface-proxy comparison could explain the remaining variation. Distance to
coast alone cannot adjudicate this. No directional signal, inconsistent signs
across months/regions, or improvement only in a pooled variance statistic
falsifies a claimed maritime solution. Preserve that outcome rather than
increasing gain until a target passes.

**Exclusions:** do not ask which metre multiplier passes the generated 1 C floor,
how to make screenshots attractive, or whether a native shoreline proves Earth
physics. Keep aridity/category and smoothing findings on their separate axis.

## Search Geometry And Evidence Policy

Use narrowing hypothesis tests, with graph tracing only for the candidate's
causal owner/vintage. Begin with the complete pinned NOAA grid and frozen
known-height residuals. Admit the smallest matching source extension: monthly
1991-2020 10 m U/V winds and surface skin temperature, with exact grid/calendar
joins. Source admission precedes a transport prototype. Open-water skin
temperature, sea ice and model-prognostic land skin values require separate
semantics; do not call every marine skin value observed SST.

Predeclare same-source-row, height-matched coastal/onshore, coastal/offshore
and interior comparisons. Keep all original 411 identities. Additional coastal
lowlands form a separate cohort: the original nine-land-cell gate excludes
immediate coasts. Report sparse support, spatial dependence and monthly-mean
wind limitations. Independent geographic holdouts supplement, not replace,
the original train/holdout split.

Verified current source/tests establish implemented behavior; repository law
and accepted intent establish permission. Scientific source metadata and
matched measurements govern physical interpretation. History explains tuning
but cannot validate it. Seek the strongest contradiction to each mechanism.
Use verified, corroborated, plausible, speculative or unresolved labels.
A code omission can be verified while its proposed physical remedy remains
plausible. Screenshots, model memory and passing authored targets do not settle
the scientific claim.

## Execution And Artifact Contract

Selected rail: bounded source admission and controlled numerical comparison,
not a broad literature report or coefficient search. Its biases are available
data and attractive correlations; require the rival comparison, original
holdouts and unchanged baseline before drawing an implementation conclusion.
The MapGen skill supplies owners, expected causal movements and disjoint proof
classes; Investigation Design supplies this question/evidence/stop contract.

Return one recommendation with a competing explanation, matched-cohort annual
and monthly results, source/unit/calendar admission, sparse/failed cases, and
the smallest supported production change or exact missing discriminator.
Retain a rerunnable Bun diagnostic and full inputs externally in the documented
Civ research directory. Extraction tools/caches never become repo dependencies.
Update this workstream's durable decision, not a second climate authority.

## Stop And Reframe

Stop on a decisive supported comparison, failed source admission or an exact
missing discriminator. Downgrade a signal that disappears after height/row
matching, lacks adequate regional support, or confuses monthly circulation
with instantaneous advection. Reframe if the temperature comparison convention
or represented horizontal/vertical support cannot carry the proposed law.
Raise only an actual product/architecture choice outside delegated intent.

No production thermal change is admitted yet. Any selected change must name
its upstream driving wind vintage; same-pass thermal-pressure-wind feedback
cannot be introduced accidentally. It then needs owner-local semantics,
independent physics and SDK review, held Huge/Standard generated cohorts and
downstream water/ecology guards. Native projection stays a separate gate.

The [scale ledger](sea-level-and-scales.md) is a parallel boundary qualification,
not another thermal forcing. Scientific height uses its admitted physical
units; generated Morphology relief remains normalized model units. Do not
fit a maritime term to conceal a height/datum mismatch, treat the native x10
display calibration as a metre conversion, or use stock `SeaLevel` variant
selection as a renderer datum. Reference-only geographic discrimination can
proceed while a generated physical scale remains unresolved.

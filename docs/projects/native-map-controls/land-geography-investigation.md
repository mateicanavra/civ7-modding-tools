## Questions And Falsifiers

**Primary:** do height-matched monthly residuals track directional ocean exposure
and ocean-land temperature contrast strongly enough to support a maritime
thermal mechanism over the strongest rival?

**Secondary:** how much is annual mean, seasonal amplitude or phase; do onshore,
offshore and interior populations differ; does the signal survive withheld
regions and retain the original lowland holdout's row-mean error?

**Rival:** heterogeneous radiative/land-surface response or the 2 m-air versus
surface-proxy comparison could explain the remaining variation. Distance to
coast alone cannot adjudicate this. No directional signal, inconsistent signs
across months/regions, or improvement only in a pooled variance statistic
falsifies a claimed maritime solution. Preserve that outcome rather than
increasing gain until a target passes.

**Exclusions:** do not ask which metre multiplier passes the generated 1 C floor,
how to make screenshots attractive, or whether a native shoreline proves Earth
physics. Keep aridity/category and smoothing findings on their separate axis.

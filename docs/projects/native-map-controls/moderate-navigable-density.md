# Moderate Navigable-River Representation

## Frame

This is one Earthlike configuration decision after the minor-river vegetation
admission repair, not a change to planetary rainfall or physical drainage.
The actor outcome is fewer terrain-replacing navigable corridors while retaining
the generated drainage network and complete selected native transitions.
The definition owner is the existing `projectRiverNetwork` authored envelope.
No domain operation, metric family, strategy, realization or SDK owner changes.

The fixed candidate changes only Earthlike `majorPercentile` from `0.88` to
`0.92`; `minorPercentile` remains `0.74`. Historical comparisons precede the
MINOR vegetation admission repair and are not the current baseline. The fresh
baseline is that merged repair with every other authored setting unchanged.

## Hypothesis And Alternatives

Raising the major discharge percentile should reduce selected NAV corridors
without deleting physical rivers, altering coast routing or changing the
physical water budget. Strongest-upstream promotion still retains coherent
selected reaches. This is a gamified representation choice, not evidence that
the Earth has a particular fraction of navigable rivers.

Keeping `0.88` is the control. Changing rainfall, lake storage or terrain to
achieve fewer rendered NAV tiles is a different physical intervention and is
not selected: none is needed to discriminate this representation setting.
There is no percentile sweep or tile quota.

major players `0..9`. Run the unchanged complete metric bank for baseline and
candidate: 22 studies, 57 scenarios including 47 Earthlike cases and the two
retained stress products. Reconcile current membership from the actual bank;

Raw-array identity belongs to the paired captures. The full-bank report proves
the declared scenario/cohort outcomes, not raw identity of arrays it does not
retain. All existing thresholds and study bindings remain unchanged.

Projected terrain, ecology, resources and starts are downstream observations,
not identity holds: demoted NAV tiles return to ordinary MINOR land terrain.
The current effective-moisture rule uses a radius-one river-class proxy:
major `+8`, minor `+4`. Demotion can therefore reduce that represented influence
while opening vegetation admission on ordinary MINOR terrain. Actual habitat,
suitability and arbitration still decide vegetation; there is no forced planting
or promotion. This study does not qualify that proxy as a resolved root-zone,
groundwater or lake-shore water balance.

The physical water supply remains identical while some modeled neighborhood
bonuses decrease. Do not interpret this as physical drying or treat the native
river class as a substitute for a future causal water-availability artifact.

## Decision And Proof Boundary

Adopt the fixed candidate only if the intended NAV reduction appears, the
paired causal guards hold, and the unchanged bank gains no failed expectation.
A loss of retained reach connectivity, legal starts, required resource supply
or other declared collateral guard refuses the candidate. Do not amend a guard
to admit it. The existing within-row temperature-variation failure remains open
even if every other expectation passes.

Contract tests and deterministic captures do not prove native visual joins or
ship movement. A later correlated native comparison must preserve the current
user game before any replacement. Until that comparison, do not describe an
owner-level change as installed, freshly rendered or gameplay-qualified.

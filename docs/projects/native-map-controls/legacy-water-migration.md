# Legacy Water Migration

## Decision

The remaining seven legacy water/projection selections are an unfinished
product migration, not demonstrated present-day solver incompatibilities.
The completed coordinator superseded the original all-open refusal reason.
Do not retain two permanent physical models merely because their evidence can
be represented by an operation union. The union in the Lotus handoff supports
actual remaining callers during migration; a certified lake never runs the
legacy equation or borrows marine masks.

The older selections use the same current operation/step SDK and artifact
publication machinery. Their difference is physical semantics and native
projection, not a license to restore old authoring patterns or duplicate
contracts. No SDK extension or calculation inside a recipe step is needed to
activate the current path.

## Current-Source Qualification

The public study bank contains 96 distinct scenarios: 47 already-certified
Earthlike cases and 49 cases across seven legacy products. Replaying all 49
with the completed stationary coordinator and authored river projection
admits every case. The cohort includes 1,255 open, seven closed and nine
subtile pool records. The current Desert Mountains cases at the historically
refused Huge1018 and Huge1538316523 seeds now admit.

Every artifact published before Hydrography's network step holds exactly,
including ground, marine mask, shelf and baseline forcing. Candidate changes
preserve authored runoff fractions, external-edge policy, confluence threshold,
physical discharge percentiles and density knob. All seven have runoffScale1
and minRunoff0, so their source law maps directly into precipitation-attributed
runoff at Number precision. Legacy lake quotas and procedural native subset
quotas are deliberately absent, not converted into new physical coefficients.

## Remaining Migration Gates

The actual remaining questions are now bounded:

1. Complete the common native water-category/height qualification. The normal
   product still uses cutoff10; the held cutoff40 diagnostic is qualified only
   for two Huge Earthlike cases. A Ring body of 252 tiles demonstrates why
   neither a guessed fixed40 nor old body-size exclusions qualify all products.
   Preserve original marine identity and physical heads; do not shrink a
   physical lake to fit a native cutoff.
2. Resolve the Archipelago identity discrepancy at its geography/forcing or
   target owner using the retained same-seed before/after pair. Do not add a
   lake quota, fallback or retry to the certified solver to satisfy it.
3. Activate the shipped profiles through existing authored water/projection
   selection, retaining each map's distinct parameters. Replay the complete
   bank, body-size/state cohort and representative native realizations.
4. Remove the displaced legacy execution, evidence unions and owned fixtures
   once supported callers migrate. Account separately for persisted/custom
   legacy configs before changing their public admission; do not silently
   reinterpret them as certified physics.

These gates replace the historical closed-basin excuse. They stay in the one
native-map-controls worktree/Graphite lineage, with one complete owner-level
change at a time. Native qualification and the held Archipelago discrimination
can be investigated independently; admission success is not native behavior
or final calibration acceptance.

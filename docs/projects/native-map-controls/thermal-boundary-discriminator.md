# Thermal Boundary Discriminator

## Frame And Decision

Current land temperature is latitude-only before ground lapse. Prescribed SST
enters wet-cell temperature but not neighboring land's thermal response. The
witnessed missing relationship is surface-boundary contrast reaching lowland
air through exchange and horizontal transport. That does not yet establish a
need to replace precipitation and soil-water dynamics.

Design one external thermal-only discriminator alongside the retained coupled
hypothesis. It is not an admitted model, a parallel production strategy or an
optimization of the four-state equations. The coupled candidate's refused
resources and unpassed annual P/E refinement obligation remain unchanged.

Select the author's offered ZEMBA **version 0 plus hydrology off** configuration,
not its active moist default. Hydrology off alone does not remove latent
transport: version 1 still transports diagnostic moist static energy.
The selected version 0 uses dry heat diffusion. Land skin and air remain
distinct; wet skin is prescribed SST. Signed evaporation/dew uses the source's
fixed availability on both signs, and its latent power is returned immediately
to air. Sensible and latent transfers cancel locally; reciprocal heat transport
cancels globally; an explicit wet thermostat accounts for fixed SST.
See the [author's primary implementation](https://github.com/daniel-francis-james-gunning/zemba/blob/ac7b8de0d600b4f61f74060b722eacd422907835/zemba.py).

The offered uniform-cloud experiment, finite two-dimensional reciprocal
geometry, prescribed SST and contemporaneous represented-domain global-air
mean are declared selections/adaptations, not claims of copying default Earth
weather. CO2 `284 ppm` is the source's preindustrial prior, not a verified
modern-NOAA forcing calibration. No wind encoding becomes SI velocity and no
normalized terrain becomes scientific altitude.

rotation and cold-initialization controls. Resolve complete periodic cycles at
the prospectively selected phase counts. Compare near-minus-far land response,
not zero total response without diffusion: the global radiative mean can still
transmit uniform warming.

wet-thermostat closure, original scalar/dense parity, periodic initialization
independence, complete-curve temporal refinement and per-cell signed annual
power refinement. Retain the 900-second/512-MiB proof limits. Increased spread
alone earns neither the production one-degree requirement nor Earth skill.
Missing source authority or a failed premise stops the hypothesis; there is no
parameter sweep, first-year substitute or automatic guard relaxation.

comparison. Only scientific and full procedural-cohort admission permit an
existing-thermal-owner implementation. It must preserve or deliberately retire
public controls, pressure samples/centering, SST vintages, one ground lapse and
independently clipped annual integration. Keep `thermalField -> climateIndices`.

PET or water artifact. A later moist selection would instead require the
already-declared joint climate/water migration.

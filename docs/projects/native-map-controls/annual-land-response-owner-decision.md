# Annual Land Response: Owner Decision

## Decision And Boundary

Design-only decision, updated October 8, 2026. The two failed scalar-coast families
remain closed. Retaining the playable approximation is legitimate interim
behavior, not completion of the delegated Earthlike convergence goal.
Neither refusal establishes that a larger water/energy model is necessary.

The [completed producer qualification](#completed-producer-qualification)
refuses the optical proposal below: current material algorithms do not
represent an exposed optical surface, and its annual air-response scale is
unqualified. This closes the proposed shortcut, not the climate objective.
The pipeline may change where a useful causal responsibility warrants it;
existing contracts and ordering are preferences, not immutable constraints.
The [next bounded design](#next-bounded-design) distinguishes an empirical
climatic predictor from a new actual-water/energy model. Neither is admitted
for fitting or implementation by this source review.

The original October 7 proposal preferred designing an **early snow-free
surface-optical prior** over a local terrain-exposure correction for the
remaining low-relief annual-temperature problem. This is a proposed smaller
expansion, not a proven minimum, executable candidate or implementation
selection. The exact unresolved choice is what represented surface supplies
that prior and how generated materials obtain defensible optical properties.
Do not start a new experiment or fit before that design exists.

## Relationship And Owner

Proposed chain: early surface character -> absorbed-shortwave contrast ->
annual land-air response -> existing pressure/moisture/demand -> water budgets
and later ecology. Each arrow is proposed, not a measured improvement.

| Quantity | Meaning, support and vintage | Owner consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surface albedo `a` | Dimensionless reflected fraction; tile-area mean; snow-free, pre-baseline prior | A surface/material producer needs an explicit optical-property contract; none is currently admitted |
| Absorptance anomaly | `(1-a) - (1-a_ref)` relative to one declared reference surface | Thermal consumes one property, not a bundled replacement climate field |
| Annual response | Candidate `k * meanQ * absorptanceAnomaly`, with `k` in C per dimensionless input | Private rule inside `compute-thermal-state`; `k` is unresolved, not inherited automatically from the solar fit |

The [solar producer](../../../plugins/mod/map/swooper-physics/src/domain/hydrology/modules/climate/ops/compute-radiative-forcing/contract.ts)
currently provides dimensionless rowwise astronomical forcing, not net W/m2.
The [substrate schema](../../../plugins/mod/map/swooper-physics/src/domain/morphology/model/atoms/substrate-fields.schema.ts)
provides erodibility and sediment depth, not reflectance. Neither quantity is
an optical substitute. Existing material ownership is a possible home, not
proof that its current inputs define the represented optical surface.

The prior must distinguish bare ground from effective vegetated surface,
declare its spectral/illumination convention and aggregation across sizes,
and exclude snow already handled by later cryosphere feedback. Deriving it
from later biomes, rainfall, erosion resistance, reference residuals or new
noise would not resolve the missing producer. No material taxonomy or optical
map is silently introduced by this decision.

[NASA](https://mynasadata.larc.nasa.gov/basic-page/albedo-values) establishes
the reflected/absorbed fraction semantics. A
[process-separated model experiment](https://doi.org/10.1175/2009JCLI3102.1)
demonstrates annual surface-temperature response to changed albedo alongside
opposing evaporation/roughness effects. This supports physical relevance,
not a transferable local air-temperature coefficient, universal net cooling,
or attribution of our residuals to albedo. Atmospheric heat convergence and
nonradiative exchange remain rivals.

## Simpler Alternative

A neighborhood terrain-exposure proxy uses already available initial relief
and a latitude pass-through. It could stay entirely inside the thermal rule.
Normalized aspect/shelter is a legitimate empirical design if its support,
resolution behavior and response are explicit; physical slope/horizon angles
cannot be inferred from uncalibrated relief and horizontal distances.

Do not select it for this problem now: equally flat, unobstructed sites offer
little terrain-exposure contrast, so broad low-relief annual relevance is
unestablished. That is not a proof of impossibility. The optical proposal can
distinguish those sites, but its additional producer and response obligations
are real. Neither alternative is scientifically qualified.

Direction-proxy exchange is not a free third option: current winds follow
thermal and pressure, and prescribed liquid SST is not qualified marine air.
Keep the rejected directed-fetch/raw-SST families closed; no new stencil or
physical-speed interpretation follows from this comparison.

## Contract, Schedule And Acceptance

An admitted static optical prior would add one input to the existing thermal
contract, preserve outputs and artifact lineage, and remain fixed across the
existing three atmosphere vintages and two ocean updates. It requires no
earlier water store, actual evaporation or new stage. Reuse the existing
single lapse, raw/clipped distinction and independent annual integration.
Apply the annual correction consistently to requested phases and dense
integration. Initially preserve zero-mean seasonal harmonics; no improved
seasonal amplitude or phase is claimed, and clipping can still alter the
published seasonal response.

This feasibility conclusion needs no new experiment. The earliest missing
handoff is surface identity -> optical prior, followed by its annual air
response magnitude. Recommend resolving that bounded input-design choice,
not building a full simulator or accepting the current limitation as final.

Physical direction/coherent regions, Earth scientific nonregression,
procedural seed/size transfer, downstream water/ecology/player effects and
native performance remain separate obligations. The existing `1-8 C` spread
guard is prospectively authored behavior, not an Earth law or validated
playability threshold; it and all scientific promotion margins remain
unchanged. More spread alone is insufficient. No production edit, experiment,
parameter search, deployment or game action is selected by this document.

## Completed Producer Qualification

Read-only source review reaches disposition **B: refuse the optical proposal
as specified**. Actual producers, rather than schema names, establish why:

- Foundation's [initial lid](../../../plugins/mod/map/swooper-physics/src/domain/foundation/modules/lithosphere/ops/compute-crust/strategies/basaltic-lid/index.ts)
  initializes bulk maturity, effective thickness, damage and support. Its
  [crust evolution](../../../plugins/mod/map/swooper-physics/src/domain/foundation/modules/orogeny/ops/compute-crust-evolution/strategies/tectonic-differentiation/index.ts)
  changes these normalized properties from tectonic events, not mineral or
  exposed-material inventories.
- [Provenance](../../../plugins/mod/map/swooper-physics/src/domain/foundation/modules/tectonics/ops/compute-tectonic-provenance/rules/index.ts)
  tracks origin/reset labels and normalized model age. Neither is weathering
  age, soil color or exposed lithology.
- [Substrate rules](../../../plugins/mod/map/swooper-physics/src/domain/morphology/modules/terrain/ops/compute-substrate/rules/index.ts)
  combine crust type/age and tectonic drivers into erodibility and sediment
  proxies. [Hillslope diffusion](../../../plugins/mod/map/swooper-physics/src/domain/morphology/modules/erosion/ops/compute-geomorphic-cycle/rules/hillslope-diffusion.ts)
  changes ground height and copies those proxies unchanged; it does not
  resolve optical exposure or a surface-material mixture.

The missing responsibility is represented exposed snow-free surface identity
and its optical assignment, followed by an independently supported annual
near-surface-air response. Missing present inputs are not a prohibition on
adding that responsibility. They do mean that repurposing resistance, sediment
or crust age is not a small supported wiring change. General albedo physics
and the existing solar gain do not supply the missing response law.

## Next Bounded Design

The relevant failure is inadequate useful regional land-temperature response,
not an inaccurate ocean solver in isolation. The current
[thermal rule](../../../plugins/mod/map/swooper-physics/src/domain/hydrology/modules/climate/ops/compute-thermal-state/rules/periodic-response.ts)
gives equal solar rows and equal heights identical land responses before later
feedback. That establishes a representation limitation, not the unique cause
of the remaining errors or proof that temperature alone cures biome banding.

Prefer one bounded design decision about a **climatic supply/demand predictor
and empirical annual land-air response** before selecting an actual-water/
energy migration. Moisture varies with circulation and relief, so it can
distinguish flat locations that scalar coast descriptions collapse. This is
a plausible input relationship, not evidence that it improves our predictions.
The [land-surface experiment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17788673/) supports
the relevance of water exchange; the [FAO formulation](https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e06.htm)
also makes clear why climatic supply/demand is not actual evapotranspiration.
Neither supplies a universal annual air-temperature coefficient for this model.

### Quantities And Ordering

| Quantity | Meaning and vintage | Owner consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Predictor rainfall and demand | Existing seasonal proxy units; incumbent thermal/atmosphere at the last prescribed SST vintage | Existing baseline climate operations; no later lakes, rivers or biomes borrowed as inputs |
| Climatic supply/demand predictor | Declared cycle-aware empirical summary on original land; not soil storage, actual ET or latent energy | Climate-domain computation, frozen for one correction; exact summary still needs admission |
| Annual land-air correction | Celsius relative to a declared reference regime; magnitude and supported domain unresolved | Existing thermal operation; one lapse and consistent sampled/dense annual application |
| Corrected final weather | Pressure/winds, rainfall and demand recomputed from corrected thermal output with the same prescribed SST | Existing climate orchestration publishes the final consumer vintage |

The [baseline](../../../plugins/mod/map/swooper-physics/src/recipes/standard/stages/hydrology/climate/baseline/steps/climate-baseline/step.ts)
currently computes moisture only after the final atmosphere. Its land
evaporation source has no water-availability input; transported humidity is a
proxy, not a conserved water budget. Moving that source earlier or renaming
it cannot create a land-water state.

The proposed ordering is one explicit lagged predictor/corrector:

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  G[Initial geography and solar forcing] --> P[Incumbent thermal and weather]
  P --> W[Climatic supply/demand predictor]
  W --> C[One empirical annual correction]
  C --> F[Recomputed final atmosphere and moisture]
  F --> H[Existing basins, incision and ecology]
  classDef prior fill:#e9f2f7,stroke:#28708d,color:#172d38;
  classDef proposed fill:#f4edcf,stroke:#8c741e,color:#332d18;
  class G,P,H prior;
  class W,C,F proposed;
```

Retain the existing two ocean updates; the correction consumes their last
prescribed SST without implicitly advancing the ocean again. This is a
bounded approximation, not a converged coupled climate. Seasonal harmonics
initially remain unchanged before clipping. The index and response belong
in domain computation, not new math in the recipe step. An invocation-local
operation result is sufficient unless cross-step reuse actually needs a
published artifact; no new stage or SDK abstraction is inherently required.

This empirical design consumes no stored water and calculates no actual ET.
It therefore adds no water debit. The existing
[basin accounting law](../../../plugins/mod/map/swooper-physics/src/domain/hydrology/modules/hydrography/model/policy/basin-water-budget.ts)
can remain unchanged, while its rainfall/demand inputs, resulting lake heads,
discharge, incision and ecology may change. If an actual supply-limited
evaporation model is selected later, its storage and once-only debit need a
separate explicit design; do not silently subtract both it and potential demand.

### Staged Proof And Stop Rule

1. **Complete the design admission.** Specify seasonal aggregation, proxy
   scaling across sizes, reference regime, annual response basis and support.
   Separate incumbent-temperature dependence in demand from new geographic
   information. Resolve this with one bounded design review, not a broad
   acquisition campaign. No independent annual response basis is established
   by the present review, so a pilot cannot start yet.
2. **One frozen candidate, if admitted.** Predeclare the law and original
   training/held/regional comparisons before any fit. Zero-response controls
   must preserve the incumbent. Scientific annual/monthly and regional guards
   precede procedural promotion; more spread alone is not useful geography.
3. **One end-to-end survivor.** Use existing fixed-geography Earth references
   only in the benchmark, then the existing paired procedural seed/size
   captures through rainfall/demand, basins, discharge, terrain and ecology.
   Publish aligned vintages and show useful regional effects in the viewer.
4. **Production qualification.** Only a surviving law earns owner integration,
   full-cohort checks and maximum-map cost proof, followed by deployment and
   fresh native/playability evidence. Preserve native water/projection
   legality; whole-map generation retains the 30-60 second player budget.

If the bounded design cannot establish the annual response basis, close this
candidate. If a frozen candidate fails, do not automatically refit, enlarge
the physical model or change thresholds. Retain the playable incumbent and
the explicitly open climate requirement. This avoids both endless theory
and premature implementation of a merely plausible mechanism. This turn
ran no fit, experiment, deployment or game action.

# Dryland Point-Water Consumer Coherence

## Outcome And Boundary

Make localized surface-water opportunities representable as lawful oasis or
watering-hole intent without requiring a whole tile to be hot, lush and fertile.
This is the next consumer unit after local plant-water response and cold-forest
coherence, not another atmospheric calibration campaign.

Keep climate, potential demand, physical drainage, lake ledgers, biome
classification and unrelated feature families unchanged. Preserve actual prior
occupancy, native biome compatibility and flat exposed-land admission. Scientific
Earth remains a benchmark, never a hidden input to the procedural recipe.

## Demonstrated Conflict

The current isolation substrate rejects any nearby broad floodplain potential,
although that proxy does not establish actual inundation or prior feature
occupancy. On the retained Standard and Huge pair it eliminates every precursor.
Removing that veto alone still admits neither feature: the current oasis response
needs at least 27.56 C annual temperature to reach the authored 0.42 floor even
with all other factors perfect; the retained land maxima are below 25.81 C.
The watering-hole response likewise has no score-admitted retained site under an
intentionally overpermissive substrate control.

This establishes consumer incompatibility on the pair, not an upstream climate
defect or the date of a historical regression. Annual heat, whole-tile plant
moisture and fertility are not each necessary conditions for localized water.
The already-admitted source/access law is documented in
[Local Plant-Water Response](local-plant-water-design.md).

## Selected Discriminator

Publish the existing annual local surface-water opportunity `L` explicitly
through the existing Hydrology `climateIndices` artifact. Do not recover it by
subtracting two rounded moisture fields or recompute routing in Ecology. Its
Float32 publication is explicit; the existing unrounded water-budget arithmetic
must remain unchanged.

Use this candidate bounded response in each existing point-water scorer:

```text
sourceSupport = L / (L + D)       when L > 0, otherwise 0
score         = drySuit(A) * sourceSupport
```

`D` is the existing published potential-demand index, in the same empirical
rainfall-index scale as `L`; `A` is unchanged climatic aridity. The family ramps
remain oasis 0.60..0.95 and watering-hole 0.45..0.85. This score is a declared
game-scale source/demand balance, not a probability, physical water allocation,
permanence measurement or validated Earth coefficient. There is no separate
annual-heat reward factor; demand still affects both climatic dryness and the
source-support balance, so the final score need not decrease as demand rises.
Fertility still affects its existing downstream consumers, not the
existence of a localized surface source.

Retire the unused isolated-water-point mask and its private radius/precursor
computation, rather than retain competing source authority. Floodplain,
hydromorphic and marine-shore substrate calculations remain for their own
consumers. Actual earlier floodplain intent still excludes occupancy.

Keep the shared Earthlike wetland admission floor at 0.42 for this discriminator.
That is an authored policy, not a physical constant. Review the score scale and
its admission together; do not lower a shared floor to conceal a failed model.

## Alternatives

- A saturated `min(1, L / D)` response makes demand-equivalent opportunity full
  support. It is a stronger supply interpretation than the smooth selected
  balance and needs its own justification, not tuning to inspected map counts.
- A vegetation-led oasis could represent a local green patch, but the current
  tile-scale biomass/fertility fields do not establish that patch's extent or
  water access. Do not silently treat them as patch observations.
- Merely removing the floodplain veto leaves demonstrated downstream failures.
  Raising climate temperature, hand-setting a compatible biome, adding quotas or
  warming the planet until a feature appears are not solutions to this unit.

## Predeclared Verification

First run producer-consistent small regimes through the actual water-budget
operation, classifier, score and legal-intent planner. Use moderate annual
temperature, contrasting fertility, ordinary imported flow and finite-body
contact. A positive DESERT/oasis and PLAINS/watering-hole regime establishes
representability, not occurrence on every generated world.

| Contrast | Required outcome |
| --- | --- |
| No source, marine donor, inaccessible elevated bank, resolved water | No invented point-water support |
| More source at fixed geometry, climate and demand | Nondecreasing bounded source score; legal intent may disappear when the unchanged classifier leaves the compatible biome |
| More contact area at fixed source | No greater per-area source support |
| Duplicate wet edges/body identity or relabeled display river class | Exact source and response invariance |
| Compatible moderate-temperature dryland, contrasting fertility | Source-led intent can survive the actual classifier and unchanged floor |
| Incompatible class/terrain or prior occupied tile | No illegal or overlapping intent |
| Cold DESERT produced by the unchanged classifier | Report source score and lawful intent separately from unmodeled seasonal liquid-water survival; no invented frost veto |
| Climate, drainage/lakes, classifier and unrelated score families | Exact hold under a retained-input comparison |

If credible positives cannot coexist with the accepted producer/classifier
equations, return that specific semantic conflict before widening scope.
Do not invent compatible class IDs in the discriminator.

After causal review, replay the retained Standard and Huge inputs before fresh
generation. Qualify the selected runtime with actual paired recipe captures,
the normal Earthlike study bank and a predeclared small variation cohort.
Explain resource/start collateral rather than promise exact final resource
identity. Retain the existing within-row thermal failure without weakening it.
Native realization is a later, separately correlated claim.

## Remaining Limits

Annual access opportunity does not establish hydroperiod, groundwater discharge,
salinity, seasonal survival or patch area. These are declared limits, not a new
solver prerequisite. Marsh, bog and mangrove response/admission questions remain
a separate wet-family unit; this intervention must not retune them.

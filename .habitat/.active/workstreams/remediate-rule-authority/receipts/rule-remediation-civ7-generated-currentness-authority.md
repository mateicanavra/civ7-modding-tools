## Retirement

The three generated-zone mutation packets and the independent provenance-label
packet are retired.

A staged-path guard cannot observe whether generated bytes were produced by
the owner command or typed by hand. Each guard therefore rejected the exact
output of its own documented recovery command. The provenance-label script was
also a partial output-text oracle over bytes that the package generator already
owns completely.

## Current Authority

- `civ7-map-policy:generated:check` derives one `GeneratedFilePlan` containing
  `civ7-tables.gen.ts`, `setup-parameters.gen.ts`, and
  `river-types.gen.d.ts`, then fails on missing, mismatched, or unexpected
  generated output.
- `civ7-map-policy:build` and `civ7-map-policy:check` depend on exact
  currentness. `civ7-types:check` delegates river-declaration currentness to
  the same map-policy owner.
- Generator-authored provenance labels are covered by exact byte currentness;
  no second string-level output validator remains.
- The `civ7-types-generated`, `civ7-map-policy-tables`, and
  `civ7-setup-parameters` host declarations remain. They continue to own path
  classification, acquisition-root exclusion, and recovery routing without
  pretending to prove mutation provenance.
- The Civ7 types generated surface is intentionally map-policy-owned because
  the map-policy generator owns the complete river declaration plan.

## Proof Boundary

Package/Nx currentness proves generated content. Habitat host declarations
classify generated paths and route recovery. Neither layer claims to observe
the human or process that last wrote byte-identical output.

The retirement is sealed by resolved rule-alias inspection, focused Habitat
registry and host-policy tests, exact package currentness, ledger count
recalculation, and diff hygiene.
